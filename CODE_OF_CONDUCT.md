# Code of conduct

We want minidash to be a welcoming project for everyone who contributes or uses it.

## Our standards
- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to the project.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone else's private information.

## Scope
This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people represent the project.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block people who break these standards. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through GitHub (@devdaviddr). Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

This code of conduct is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
